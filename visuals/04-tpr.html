FIG 3  ·  Transitive Privilege Ratio · structural · model-independent

Declared scope is the menu. Reachable scope is the meal.

TPR is the ratio of consequential actions reachable via tool composition to those declared at the OAuth boundary. Across 30 POC scenarios: mean 1.43×, peak 3.00×. The full benchmark adds a fourth simulator and 100+ endpoints — median TPR is expected to comfortably exceed 3.

Worked example · T2-0009 (peak TPR scenario)
1 declared action → 3 reachable consequential tools
The access review names itsm.update_incident. The granted scopes also reach incident creation and outbound email. TPR = 3 / 1 = 3.00×.
